# CryoQuantum-Optics-Codex (低温量子光学测试系统手册)

> **Version: 3.0.0**
>
> A single-file, fully offline and interactive handbook and equipment maintenance system for low-temperature quantum-optical spectroscopy of two-dimensional materials.

---

## 🌟 Features & Highlights

- **Single-File Interactive Handbook**: [`低温量子光学测试系统手册.html`](低温量子光学测试系统手册.html) is self-contained with zero external dependencies.
- **Publication-Style Inline SVG Diagrams**: Confocal PL and exciton recombination, Stokes / Anti-Stokes Raman scattering, waveplate polarization paths, and K/K′ valley-selective optical transitions.
- **Experimental & Theoretical Modules**: PL, Raman, differential reflectance, linear/circular polarization, 4-channel valley measurements, valley Zeeman spectroscopy, fitting uncertainty and quality control.
- **Analytical Offline Toolbox**: Interactive tools for DOCP/DOLP, excitonic g-factor, power law fitting, photon energy conversion, and standardized filename generator.
- **Responsive Themes**: Dark/light modes with print/PDF support.

---

## 🛠️ Equipment Operation & Maintenance SOP (attoDRY 2100 设备维护与洗气规范)

Located in [`Equipment gas washing and maintenance/`](Equipment%20gas%20washing%20and%20maintenance/):
- **`低温设备使用手册.html`**: Comprehensive offline SOP manual for attocube attoDRY 2100 cryostat & superconducting magnet operations, gas washing, vacuum regeneration, and cooldown.
- **`低温设备操作速查卡_简化版.html`**: Compact quick-reference card tailored for laboratory bench execution.
- **`低温设备使用手册.pptx`**: Original training slide deck and hardware/pipeline schematic illustrations.
- **8-Step Gas Washing Protocol**: Standardized procedures for warmup, turbopump relocation, trap degassing at 8h, deep vacuuming at 12h, 3×N₂ + 1×He gas cycles, buffer dump refill (950 mbar), and automated cooldown to 1.65 K.

## 🔬 Measurement Philosophy

Every physical conclusion should remain traceable to the optical state, sample coordinate system, excitation power, collection geometry, calibration record, fitting model and uncertainty definition.
